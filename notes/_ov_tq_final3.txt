usage: peer_worktree.py [-h] [--root ROOT]
                        {list,spawn,ensure-pool,add,remove} ...

Helpers for parallel-peer git worktrees. peer_loop inventories trees each
continuous cycle; add/remove stay opt-in.

positional arguments:
  {list,spawn,ensure-pool,add,remove}
    list                List worktrees (git worktree list --porcelain)
    spawn               Spawn one parallel-peer worktree (optional --slot or
                        --label)
    ensure-pool         Ensure .worktrees/peer-0..N for 8+ parallel peers
    add                 Add a worktree (optional --dry-run)
    remove              Remove a worktree (dry-run by default; never --force)
